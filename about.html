ABOUT SeekScholarship

Helping Students Find Their Opportunity.

SeekScholarship is a scholarship discovery platform designed to help students find educational opportunities around the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make scholarship information easier to discover, understand and access, especially for students who may not have access to reliable educational resources.

What We Provide

We organize scholarships and educational opportunities by country, degree, funding type and other important criteria so students can find opportunities that match their goals.

How SeekScholarship Works

Browse opportunities by country or degree, use the search filters to narrow the list, then open a scholarship page to review its details and application link. Applications are completed with the official scholarship provider.

Explore study funding opportunities and take your next step toward an education abroad.

Disclaimer & information

Scholarship details can change. SeekScholarship is an independent discovery resource and is not a scholarship provider. Confirm eligibility, deadlines, and application instructions with the official organization before applying.
